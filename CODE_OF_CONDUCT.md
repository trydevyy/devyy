# Code of conduct

Help make Devyy a welcoming project. Treat people with respect, give specific and constructive feedback, and assume that contributors bring different experiences and levels of expertise. Keep discussions focused on the work and respect requests for privacy.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, and sharing someone else's private information without permission are unacceptable in project spaces.

Report concerns privately to a repository maintainer. Maintainers may remove content, warn participants, or restrict participation when conduct harms the community. Reports will be handled with discretion.
